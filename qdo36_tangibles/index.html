Tangible input for Quai des Orfèvres 36.

Talks to the tangible-objects daemon over its GraphQL interface (pattern registration, tangibleEvents subscription, deviceInfo and touchPoints queries) and derives the landmark anchor pose plus the map-space transform from it.
