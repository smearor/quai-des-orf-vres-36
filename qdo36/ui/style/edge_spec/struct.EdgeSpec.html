Expand description

One rendered edge - endpoints already in widget px, plus the domain data the style may color by.

Fields§

§from: Vec2<Pixels>

From-station position (px).

§to: Vec2<Pixels>

To-station position (px).

§transport: Transport

The edge’s transport mode.

§line: Option<&'a LineName>

Transit line (metro only; colored per [map.lines]).

§map: &'a GameMap

The map - line-color lookup.

§route: Vec<Vec2<Pixels>>

The drawn polyline in px (routed through intermediate stations); empty means a straight from -> to segment.

§offsets: Vec<Pixels>

Perpendicular pixel offset per drawn segment - parallel metro lines shift sideways so every line keeps its own color; one entry per drawn segment, empty means centered.

§smooth: bool

Draw the route as a smooth spline through its interior points (Catmull-Rom) with straight first/last hops - the Batobus boat course curves along the river, the pier stubs stay straight.

§dashed: bool

Draw the edge dashed with a dark rim - metro lanes in the combined parallel+metro overlay, so a line color matching a neighbor lane (M1 vs taxi, M6/M12 vs bus) stays readable.

§wavy: bool

Draw the edge as a sine wave along its route - the Batobus boat wake on the water.

§wave_phase: Millimeters

Arc-length phase offset of the wave, anchored to the water course - edges sharing a stretch trace identical waves instead of two crossing ones.

§highlight: bool

Whether the edge draws in its transport/line color; false draws the neutral edge ink (standard view).

§px_per_mm: PixelsPerMm

Pixels per surface millimeter.
