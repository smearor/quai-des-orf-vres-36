Functions§

smooth_path 🔒
A smooth spline through points (Catmull-Rom -> cubic Bézier) - the Batobus boat course curves along the river between piers. The first and last hop draw straight: they are the short pier stubs off the waterline, and the off-water station may not pull the spline tangents or the curve loops at every pier.
wavy_path 🔒
A sine wave traced along points - the Batobus boat wake: the polyline is sampled at fine arc-length steps and each sample shifts perpendicular to the local segment direction. phase offsets the wave’s arc position, so edges sharing a stretch trace the same wave.
