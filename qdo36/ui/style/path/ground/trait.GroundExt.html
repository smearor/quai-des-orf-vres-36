Expand description

Cairo ground fills: ctx.fill_ground(..) floods the city’s ground mask, ctx.tile_fill(..) repeats the outer texture.

fn fill_ground(&self, ground: &Ground, color: &Color<f64>, to_px: ToPx<'_>)

Fills every ground polygon with color - the city rests on plain paper while the countryside keeps its texture.

fn tile_fill( &self, tile: &ImageSurface, pin: &Vec2<Pixels>, width: f64, height: f64, )

Fills the whole widget with tile repeated, anchored so the tile origin sits at pin - the texture repeats infinitely and pans with the map.
