Expand description

Cairo building paths: ctx.house_*_path(..) appends roof outlines and details, ctx.draw_houses(..) draws the block’s houses, ctx.district_label(..)/ctx.park_label(..) place the place captions.

Required Methods§

Source

fn house_outline_path(&self, footprint: &PxPolygon<'_>)

Appends a house footprint’s closed outline to the current path - for the fill and the outline stroke.

Source

fn house_detail_path(&self, footprint: &PxPolygon<'_>, units: Units)

Appends a house’s top-view roof details to the current path: the footprint shrunk toward its centroid reads as the roof body, quads get the hip diagonals from each outer corner to its inner corner, and terraced houses (units

  1. get split lines across their front.
Source

fn iso_wall_path(&self, quad: &PxPolygon<'_>, shift: Vec2<Pixels>)

Appends the outward-facing wall quads of an iso-extruded footprint: each edge’s side extends by shift when its outward normal leans toward the shift direction - the far side hides under the roof.

Source

fn place_label( &self, name: &str, pos: Vec2<Millimeters>, to_px: ToPx<'_>, px_per_mm: PixelsPerMm, style: &CaptionStyle, )

Centers name in italic serif at pos (map mm): a wide halo stroke around the glyph outlines lifts the text off its background, then the glyphs fill in ink.

Source

fn draw_houses( &self, block: &BlockSpec<'_>, palette: &StylePalette, to_px: ToPx<'_>, )

Draws the block’s houses in block.house_mode: flat top-down footprints, a fixed up-left isometric extrusion, or a radial one where every roof shifts toward the map center so the walls face the nearest table edge. The fill shade varies with width and variant in every mode.

Source

fn district_label( &self, label: &DistrictLabel, to_px: ToPx<'_>, px_per_mm: PixelsPerMm, style: &CaptionStyle, )

Draws a block’s district name centered at its deepest inside point, in italic serif with a halo outline so it stays readable over houses and streets.

Source

fn park_label( &self, name: &str, pos: Vec2<Millimeters>, to_px: ToPx<'_>, px_per_mm: PixelsPerMm, style: &CaptionStyle, )

Draws a park’s name centered at its blob, smaller than the district caption.
