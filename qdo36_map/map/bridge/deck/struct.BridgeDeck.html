Expand description

The drawn bridge of a bridge-kind station: a deck perpendicular to the river, spanning bank to bank. Two cases: on_water stations sit on the water - the deck centers on the station marker and above-ground (taxi/bus) edges dock at its ends. Bridgehead stations stand on a bank - the deck centers on the water below them and their edges stay ordinary roads.

Fields§

§center: Vec2<Millimeters>

Deck center (map mm): the station position when on_water, its projection on the river course for bridgeheads.

§axis: Vec2<Millimeters>

Unit deck axis: perpendicular to the river course.

§half_span: Millimeters

Half the deck length.

§on_water: bool

The station itself is the bridge (marker mid-river) - only those decks take edge attachments.

pub fn dock_start(&self, route: &mut Polyline)

Docks a batobus route’s first point onto this deck: drops the course points inside the bend zone (the raw route would otherwise overshoot to the dock’s projection and double back - a hairpin), then splices two waypoints upstream of the deck center along the river tangent, part way toward the bank-side dock end.
