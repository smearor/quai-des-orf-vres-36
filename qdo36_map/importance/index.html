Strategic importance of a station for movement-heavy roles.

Score formula (documented, tuning-friendly): 3 * reach + 2 * transports + degree

  • reach: distinct stations reachable in one move - how far the fugitive escapes per turn
  • transports: served transports count - taxi-only stations are the least important
  • degree: outgoing edges - raw branching factor
