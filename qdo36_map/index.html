qdo36-map - the Paris (and future cities) game map domain.

Loads a curated TOML dataset into a validated station graph (GameMap): stations, undirected transport edges, coordinate bounds in map millimeters, city rule defaults and the city’s anchor landmark spec. No GTK, no daemon - pure domain model.
