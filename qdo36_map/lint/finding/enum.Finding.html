Expand description

A dataset quality violation.

Variants§

§

Crossing

Two edges of the same layer cross without a shared station (Q1: each transport net is planar; metro lines may cross each other, only same-line self-crossings report).

Fields

§transport: Transport

The layer the crossing sits on.

§first: EdgePair

First edge.

§second: EdgePair

Second edge.

§

Disconnected

A transport’s adjacency graph is split into islands (Q2).

Fields

§transport: Transport

The transport layer.

§components: ComponentCount

How many components the net has.

§sizes: Vec<StationCount>

Stations per component, sorted descending.

§

LineNotLinear

A metro line is not a simple path (Q3).

Fields

§line: LineName

The failing line.

§cause: LinearityCause

Why it fails.

§

BatobusNotLinear

The batobus pier chain is not a simple path (Q7).

Fields

§cause: LinearityCause

Why it fails.

§

IsolatedLine(LineName)

A metro line shares no station with any other line - no transfer possible (Q4).

§

MetroStationUnserved

A metro station lacks taxi and/or bus service (Q5).

Fields

§station: StationId

The underserviced station.

§missing: MissingService

Which services are missing.

§

BusCoverageBelow

Fewer than 90% of bus stations also have taxi service (Q6).

Fields

§taxi_covered: StationCount

Bus stations with taxi service.

§bus_total: StationCount

All bus stations.

§

OrphanedStation(StationId)

A station has no edges at all - unreachable from anywhere.

§

LeafStation(StationId)

A station has exactly one edge overall - a dead end that traps whoever enters (Q9, informational).

§

DuplicatePosition(StationId, StationId)

Two stations sit on the same position (Q9).

§

StationOnEdge

A station lies almost on an edge it does not belong to - the route visually runs through the station without serving it, so the direct edge is hard to tell from the detour via the station (Q14, readability).

Fields

§transport: Transport

The transport layer the offending edge belongs to.

§edge: EdgePair

The edge passing the station.

§station: StationId

The station sitting on the line.

§distance_mm: Millimeters

Perpendicular distance in map millimeters.

§

UnderconnectedStart

A start-deal station has fewer than two edges - a dealt detective would start trapped (Q10).

Fields

§station: StationId

The weak start station.

§degree: StationDegree

Its total edge degree.

§

DensityInversion

The density order is inverted: a sparser-class layer carries more edges than a denser one, expected taxi >= bus >= metro (Q11, soft).

Fields

§sparser: Transport

The layer that should be sparser but is denser.

§denser: Transport

The layer that should be denser.
