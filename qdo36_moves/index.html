qdo36-moves - the move domain of Quai des Orfèvres 36.

Move vocabulary (Hop, MoveKind, MoveRequest, MoveRecord, MoveError) plus game lifecycle types (GamePhase, GameOutcome, GameLog, PublicEntry, RevealSchedule). Pure domain, no session orchestration.
